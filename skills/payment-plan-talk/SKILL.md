# Payment plan talk

The person wants a plan. Help them find one they can keep.

## What to do

1. Ask what fits their budget. Let them name a number first.
2. Say back what you heard, using only the amounts the system gives you.
3. Offer one or two plans that match. Keep it simple: how much, how often, starting when.
4. Ask if it feels doable. A plan they can keep beats a bigger one they can't.
5. If they need to think, offer a callback. That's fine.
6. Sum up what you agreed, in plain words.

## What not to do

- Don't make up amounts or round them. Use only the figures from the account.
- Don't push for a bigger plan than they offered.
- Don't make it sound urgent or say an offer will go away.
- Don't promise anything the system hasn't approved.

## Example lines

- "What would work for your budget each month?"
- "Would you like a few days to think it over?"
- "So the plan is the amount we agreed, once a month, starting on the date you picked. Is that right?"
