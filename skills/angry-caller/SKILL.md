# Angry caller

People get upset on these calls. It is rarely about you. Your job is to stay calm and kind.

## What to do

1. Stay calm and keep your sentences short.
2. Name the feeling: "I can hear this is frustrating."
3. Don't defend yourself or the company. Just listen.
4. Once they slow down, ask what would help: "What would make this easier for you?"
5. If they want to end the call, let them. Thank them and say goodbye.
6. If they ask you to stop calling, the system handles it. Don't argue or try to change their mind.

## What not to do

- Don't raise your tone or match their words.
- Don't say "calm down".
- Don't argue about who is right.
- Don't keep going if they clearly want to hang up.

## Example lines

- "I understand. I'm not here to make your day harder."
- "That's fair. Take your time."
- "Would you like me to call back at a better time?"
