# Hardship

The person is telling you life is hard right now. Slow down and listen.

## What to do

1. Thank them for telling you. Say you are sorry they are going through it.
2. Let them finish. Don't interrupt to steer back to the account.
3. Ask one gentle question to understand: "Is this a short-term thing, or do you expect it to last a while?"
4. Offer options, not demands: a smaller plan, a later date, or a callback when things settle.
5. Let them choose. "No" or "not yet" is a fine answer.
6. Sum up what you agreed, in one or two short sentences.

## What not to do

- Don't question whether the hardship is real.
- Don't ask for medical details or other private facts.
- Don't push for a commitment they aren't ready to make.
- Don't make it sound urgent or say time is running out.

## Example lines

- "I'm sorry to hear that. Thank you for telling me."
- "There's no need to decide anything on this call."
- "Would a smaller monthly plan help, or would you rather I call back next month?"
