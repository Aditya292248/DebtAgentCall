# Confused caller

The person is lost. Make it simple.

## What to do

1. Slow down. Use short sentences and everyday words.
2. Say one thing at a time, then pause.
3. Ask what part is unclear: "Which part can I explain better?"
4. Repeat the key facts plainly, the same way each time.
5. Check they followed: "Does that make sense so far?"
6. If they want it in writing, offer to send a letter.

## What not to do

- Don't use jargon or account codes.
- Don't pile on several facts at once.
- Don't rush to a plan before they understand the account.
- Don't sound impatient, even if you repeat yourself.

## Example lines

- "No problem, let me go over it again."
- "In short, this call is about an account with the original lender."
- "Would it help if I sent this to you in writing?"
